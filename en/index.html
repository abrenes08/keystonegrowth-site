A few things worth knowing

Clear from
the beginning.

A direct relationship starts with clear expectations.

Who is a good fit for Keystone?

Businesses with a clear offer, capacity for more customers, and someone ready to respond to inquiries. We start by understanding your sales process and deciding whether the scope makes sense.

Do I have to buy all three services?

No. We can work on advertising, a website or landing page, or CRM automation individually. When connecting them solves a real problem, we build a combined scope.

Will I work directly with Aaron?

Yes. Aaron leads strategy, communication, and quality review. Automation and specialist delivery partners may support execution, while Aaron remains your point of contact.

Do you personally call or close my leads?

Human lead handling and sales are not included in the standard scope. Automation can support acknowledgment, reminders, and nurture; your team handles live conversations and closing unless separately agreed.

How much does it cost?

We quote based on the deliverables, channels, integrations, and ongoing support your business needs. Management and project fees are separate from advertising spend, software, and messaging costs.

Can we work in Spanish?

Absolutely. Keystone works in English and Spanish. Choose the language you prefer, and we can adapt the website, campaigns, and follow-up to your audience.
