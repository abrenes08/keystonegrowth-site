For teams losing track between the inquiry and the sale

If contacts sit in separate inboxes, reminders depend on memory, or no one knows who should reply, the first priority is a practical process. We map your current tools and sales stages before adding software or automation.

Start with a pipeline people can use

We define stages such as new inquiry, contacted, appointment booked, and closed around your actual process. Ownership and next actions matter more than building a complicated dashboard no one updates.

Automate the repeatable work

Depending on your tools and permissions, workflows can acknowledge an inquiry, assign a lead, create a task, send a booking reminder, or run a follow-up sequence. Channel availability and message permissions are confirmed before anything is enabled.

Keep a human path through the process

Automation should support the team’s work. We agree where staff take over, how replies are handled, and what stops a sequence. Your team remains responsible for live sales conversations unless a different scope is agreed.

What a scope can include

  • Lead journey and tool review
  • CRM pipeline and field setup
  • Form routing, tasks, and booking workflows
  • Agreed follow-up sequences and handoff documentation

Questions before getting started

Do you call and close my leads?

Human lead handling and closing are not included in the standard scope. Automation supports the process; your team handles conversations and sales unless separately agreed.

Can you use my current CRM?

We first review its capabilities, access, and available integrations. We propose changes based on what your existing setup can support.

Are software and messaging costs included?

No. Licenses, email or SMS usage, and third-party integration costs are separate from project or management fees and are identified in the proposal.
